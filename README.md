# Pico Dhaka

Mexican-inspired food, made your way in Dhaka. Order online and find us at occasional events.

## Visit Pico

**Website and menu:** [pico-dhaka.vercel.app](https://pico-dhaka.vercel.app/)

Open **View menu** to see the current burrito options and prices. A flour tortilla is Tk 45; the full burrito is Tk 400. Use **Order now** to message Pico, or **Check delivery areas** to explore the Dhaka map and confirm delivery to your address.
